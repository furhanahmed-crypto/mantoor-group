About Nandan Nest

Nandan Nest is thoughtfully crafted for young families and first-time homebuyers who refuse to compromise on quality. Located in the rapidly developing micro-market of Chitkul, Hyderabad, these elegant simplex villas deliver a complete, comfortable lifestyle within a smartly designed single-floor plan.

Every detail of Nandan Nest speaks to Mantoor's philosophy of thoughtful design — from the warm, sunlit interiors to the well-planned community spaces that encourage neighbours to become lifelong friends.

Chitkul's excellent connectivity to HITEC City, Gachibowli, and the Outer Ring Road makes this project a smart investment choice as well as a wonderful place to call home.
