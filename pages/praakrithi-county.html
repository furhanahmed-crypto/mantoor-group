About Praakrithi County Villas

Presenting Mantoor Praakrithi County Villas — a premium new launch from Mantoor Group, set across an expansive 13-acre gated community next to the ORR Service Road at Rameshwar Banda, Beeramguda. Crafted for families who value space, privacy, and refined living, this landmark project brings together elegant triplex villas with the trusted quality of the Mantoor name.

With only 193 exclusive units, Praakrithi County offers a rare opportunity to own a spacious triplex home starting from 2,500 sq.ft — available in both east and west facing orientations to suit your lifestyle and preferences.

Strategically positioned beside the Outer Ring Road, the project places you minutes from Patancheru, Miyapur, and Hyderabad's leading IT corridors — making it an exceptional choice for both end-use and long-term investment at a special introductory price.
