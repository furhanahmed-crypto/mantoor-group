About Nandan Emerald

Nandan Emerald is Mantoor Group's flagship triplex villa community set amidst the lush, serene landscapes of Wadakpally-Beeramguda. Designed for the modern Indian family that cherishes togetherness while valuing privacy, these expansive triplex villas offer a rare combination of generous living space and thoughtful design.

Each villa is crafted with premium materials, superior finishes, and attention to every architectural detail — giving you a home that doesn't just look beautiful on day one, but holds its quality and value for generations to come.

Situated in one of Hyderabad's fastest-growing corridors, Nandan Emerald places your family close to top schools, hospitals, retail hubs, and the IT corridors of HITEC City and Gachibowli, while still offering the peaceful, green environment your family deserves.
