About Mantoor Mukunda

Mantoor Mukunda is a landmark villa township in the emerging locality of Velmala, Hyderabad — Mantoor Group's most versatile project to date. Offering both duplex and triplex configurations within the same gated community, Mukunda gives every family the freedom to choose the home that perfectly fits their life.

The duplex villas are ideal for nuclear families seeking spacious, modern living across two floors, while the triplex options cater beautifully to joint families wanting to live together across generous floor plans that still give everyone their own space and privacy.

Velmala's strategic location — well-connected to Hyderabad's IT corridors and the Outer Ring Road — combined with the promise of the Mantoor name makes this project one of the most sought-after addresses in the city today.
